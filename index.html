• Paint Layers Mode: Use standard drawing tools (Pencil, Eraser, Bucket, Dropper) to design master sprite layers.
• Animate Clips Mode: Frame-by-frame puppet deformation. Offsets are strictly masked to the layer marked as [EDIT].
• Variants & Cascading: Create variants to reskin or add accessory layers. Base motion cascades automatically.
