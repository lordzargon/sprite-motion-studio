Quick Guide & Shortcuts (Version 2)

Dual Mode Workflow

• Paint Layers Mode: Use standard drawing tools (Pencil, Eraser, Bucket, Dropper) to design the master layers of your sprite.
• Animate Clips Mode: Frame-by-frame deformation. Deformations are strictly masked by whichever layer is marked as [EDIT] in the Layers panel.
• Variants & Cascading Animations: Create variants to reskin or add layers (e.g. Ponytail, Cape). Base animations cascade down to variants automatically unless locally overridden.
